Standard ASR models struggle in multi-speaker trilingual environments, often silence-padding sub-1s audio clips into fabricated captions or misattributing speaker turns.

Laxmi built a real-time pipeline combining VAD segmentation, SpeechBrain ECAPA-TDNN voice embeddings, and 4 custom hallucination guards to reduce false transcriptions to near zero without discarding real speech.
