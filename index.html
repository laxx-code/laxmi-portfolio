01 // PROBLEM DEFINITION

"The problem wasn't transcription. It was knowing when to trust the transcription."

Standard ASR pipelines can behave unpredictably in multi-speaker environments, especially with short utterances, silence, overlapping speech, language switching, and frequent speaker changes.

A particularly important failure mode occurs when sub-1-second or near-silent audio segments are passed directly to Whisper. Instead of returning no speech, the model can sometimes generate plausible-looking but fabricated captions.

FAILURE PROPAGATION CHAIN
Wrong Audio Segment → Fabricated Caption
↓
Wrong Speaker Attribution → Misattributed Turn
↓
Wrong Meeting Summary → Fabricated Action Items

The goal was therefore not simply to transcribe audio, but to determine which audio segments should reach ASR and maintain reliable speaker attribution across multilingual conversations.

05 // VALIDATION LAYER

"Four layers between audio and trusted text"

01 // SPEECH PRESENCE graphic_eq

VAD Energy Gate

VAD determines whether meaningful speech exists before the segment reaches ASR.

Goal: Prevent silence and non-speech audio from becoming text.
02 // SEGMENT VALIDATION timer

Duration & Sub-1s Filter

Extremely short or unreliable segments are handled carefully instead of blindly sending everything to Whisper.

Goal: Reduce unreliable predictions from sub-1-second audio.
03 // LANGUAGE RESTRICTION translate

Constrained Language Decoding

Whisper inference is constrained according to the expected meeting language (English / Hindi / Marathi).

Goal: Reduce implausible language switching & hallucinated translations.
04 // OUTPUT VALIDATION verified_user

Confidence & Repetition Check

ASR output is evaluated before entering the final transcript. Suspicious or low-confidence predictions are rejected.

Goal: Prevent unreliable model output from contaminating meeting intelligence.
06 // PARAMETER CALIBRATION

"Optimizing for real conversations, not perfect laboratory audio"

Thresholds were tuned using systematic error analysis around speech/no-speech detection, segment duration, speaker similarity, transcription confidence, ambiguous Hindi/Marathi segments, short utterances, and noisy/incomplete speech.

INTERACTIVE SYSTEM PARAMETER TUNING
VAD Energy Cutoff -35dB
Suppresses background ambient noise floor
Min Segment Duration 0.40s
Filters sub-second transient pops and clicks
ECAPA Distance Threshold 0.68
Cosine similarity threshold for speaker identity matching
ASR Log-Prob Floor -0.85
Rejects low-confidence decoded tokens
"Reject unreliable segments without accidentally throwing away genuine speech."
07 // RATIONALE & TRADE-OFFS

Engineering Decision Log

WHY VAD BEFORE WHISPER? Prevent silence and non-speech background audio from ever reaching the ASR model state.
WHY ECAPA-TDNN? Provides robust 192-dimensional acoustic representations for precise voice identification across speakers.
WHY WHISPER? Strong pre-trained multilingual ASR foundation for English, Hindi, and Marathi code-switched dialogues.
WHY LANGUAGE RESTRICTION? Eliminates random language drift and implausible language switching on short meeting segments.
WHY MULTIPLE GUARDS? Different failure modes (silence, duration, language, repetition) require distinct specialized validation signals.
